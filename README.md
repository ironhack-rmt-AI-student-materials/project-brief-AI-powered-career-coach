# Project | AI-Powered Career Coach

<br>

## Goal

Build an AI-powered Career Coach that helps users prepare for job interviews, improve their CVs, and advance their careers through a conversational interface.

## Core Requirements

Your application should include, at the very least, the following features:

### 1. Mock Interview Mode

Build a text-based AI interviewer that simulates a real job interview tailored to the user's target role:

- Interview Setup: Users can specify the role they are preparing for and optionally provide a job offer or job description to tailor the interview to the position's requirements.

- Conduct the Interview: The AI should ask one question at a time and wait for the user's answer before continuing. It should ask relevant follow-up questions based on the user's responses, adapting the conversation to simulate a realistic interview.

- Interview Feedback: Once the interview is complete, provide an overall performance score, highlight the user's strengths and weaknesses, and suggest concrete ways to improve their answers and interview performance.

### 2. CV Reviewer Mode

Build an AI-powered CV reviewer that helps users improve their CV for a specific role.

- Allow users to upload their CV (or paste its contents as text) + provide info about the role they're applying for.
- Analyze the CV and provide personalized, actionable feedback on content, clarity, formatting, errors, relevant skills, and areas for improvement.

### 3. Application & Deployment

Build and deploy an application that integrates both features, allowing users to practice interviews and submit CVs for review.

If you have additional time, here are some extra challenges to take your project even further:

<details>
<summary>Job Search & Discovery</summary>

Implement the functionality required to allow the agent to provide relevant job offers (e.g., using web search and/or integrating with a third-party API)

</details>

<details>
<summary>Job-Fit Analysis & Skills Gap Assessment</summary>

Allow users to compare their CV against a specific job offer. Identify relevant and missing skills, provide a job-match score, and suggest improvements.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Personalized Learning Roadmap</summary>

Generate a personalized learning plan based on the user's target role, CV gaps, and/or interview performance.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Company-Specific Interview Preparation</summary>

Allow users to prepare for interviews at a specific company and role. Use web search to gather relevant information about the company, its values, and potential interview topics.
</details>

<details>
<summary>LLM-as-a-Judge Evaluation</summary>

Implement an evaluation system that uses an LLM to assess CV reviews against predefined criteria, such as factual accuracy, relevance, actionability, and completeness. Return structured scores and justifications.
</details>
